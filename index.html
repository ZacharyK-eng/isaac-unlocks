EXchive .dat exporter — validated test subset

This exporter runs entirely on your phone using the bundled Repentance+ reference save. Character unlock statuses are saved in JSON and shown in the audit, but new manual character unlocks cannot yet be written to the binary; those selections block export. It supports the three verified Isaac marks and individually selected secret IDs. Other marks and challenges are not yet mapped; export is blocked when those are selected so it cannot silently misrepresent your progress.
